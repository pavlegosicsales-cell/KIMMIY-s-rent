  • FAQ

Not sure yet? Have some questions? We listed the ones most frequently asked.

Do you deliver to Belgrade Airport?

Yes, around the clock. We are five minutes from Nikola Tesla Airport, so we meet you at arrivals with the car ready. Send us your flight number and we track the landing.

What do I need to rent a car?

A valid driving licence held for at least two years, a passport or ID card, and a minimum age of 23. That is all. We handle the paperwork at handover in a few minutes.

Is insurance included in the price?

Yes. Every rental includes full insurance and 300 kilometres per day. A refundable deposit of 500 euros is held at pick-up and returned when you bring the car back.

Can I drive outside Serbia?

Cross-border trips are possible with advance notice. Tell us your route when you book and we arrange the green card insurance and border documents for the car.

How do I pay?

Card or cash, whichever suits you. You pay at handover, and the deposit is released the moment we check the car back in at drop-off.

Ready to Hit the Road in Style?

Call or message us with your flight details and the car you want. We confirm in minutes and meet you at arrivals, your hotel or any address in Belgrade.
