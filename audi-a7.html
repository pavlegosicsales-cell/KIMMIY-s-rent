Overview

The A7 Sportback pairs a 3.0 TFSI V6 with quattro all-wheel drive, so it holds the road in rain, on mountain passes and on the long straights of the A1. Inside you get Valcona leather, a Bang & Olufsen sound system and a boot that swallows three large suitcases without folding a seat.

Vehicle features

Every car in our fleet is a 2017 model kept in showroom condition. The A7 comes with the equipment business travellers actually use:

  • Quattro all-wheel drive
  • Adaptive cruise control
  • Apple CarPlay and Android Auto
  • MMI navigation with live traffic
  • Rear-view camera and parking sensors
  • Heated and ventilated front seats
  • Keyless entry and start
  • 20-inch alloy wheels
What is included
  • Full insurance and 300 km a day
  • Delivery to Belgrade Airport arrivals, day or night
  • Second driver at no extra cost
