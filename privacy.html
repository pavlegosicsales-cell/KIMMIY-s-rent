Privacy Policy

Kimmiy’s Rent, Surčin, Belgrade, operates this website. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

When you contact us by phone, email or a form on this site, we collect your name, phone number, email address and the details of your enquiry, such as rental dates and flight number.

How we use it

We use your details only to respond to your enquiry, prepare your rental agreement and arrange handover of the car. We do not sell or share your information with third parties.

Storage and deletion

Enquiry details are kept only as long as needed to handle your booking and meet legal record-keeping duties. You can ask us to delete your details at any time by emailing bookings@kimmiysrent.com.

Cookies

This site does not set tracking cookies.
